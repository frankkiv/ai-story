AI grew hands.
From chat to agents.

AI went from a consultant that talks to a team of assistants that do the work, and you can direct them from your phone. But it isn't actually "learning" you. The model itself never changes; what changes is the set of "notes" handed to it every time, which engineers call the context.

For family · The practical part

So what should you watch out for when using AI?

In daily life it comes down to these five things. The full explanations, a version you can forward, and the questions parents ask most are on the "For family" page.

  1. Anything involving money or passwords, check it yourself
  2. Don't paste in ID numbers, bank accounts or medical records
  3. Take a look at the privacy settings
  4. Double-check numbers, drug names and legal questions
  5. Be suspicious of "AI investing" or "AI trading" offers
