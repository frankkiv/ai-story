PART 1 · Timeline

From talking,
to doing

From late 2022 to now, AI took four big steps: consultant → tradesperson → butler → general contractor. Scroll down to watch each event appear along the timeline. What changed under the hood at each step is explained on the How it works page.

Every major lab's key releases are included, but agent tool examples mostly use Claude Code, which the author uses daily, so Anthropic events are slightly overrepresented.

2022
  2. ConsultantYou ask, it answersFrom late 2022, AI got very good at answering questions, but you still did the work yourself.
  3. 2022.11Model

    ChatGPT launches

    Many people chat with an AI for the first time. It can only "talk": it gives you text or code, and you copy, paste and run it yourself.

    In plain wordsLike phoning a very knowledgeable consultant who won't come over to fix your plumbing.

  4. 2023.03ModelGPT-4 and the first ClaudeThe large-model race officially begins.
  5. 2023.12ModelGoogle launches GeminiThe big three take shape.
  6. 2024.05ModelGPT-4o: real-time voice and visionAI starts to look at photos and hold spoken conversations.
  7. 2024.09Model

    Reasoning models arrive (OpenAI o1)

    The model writes out a long chain of thinking before answering. That's a prerequisite for letting AI carry out multi-step work on its own.

    In plain wordsFrom the student who blurts out answers to the one who drafts on scrap paper first.

  8. TradespersonIt starts doing the workFrom late 2024, AI gets tools and works directly on your computer.
  9. 2024.10Agent

    Claude learns to use a computer (computer use)

    It looks at the screen, moves the mouse and types. Still slow and often clicks the wrong thing, but for the first time AI has hands inside a computer.

  10. 2024.11Agent

    Anthropic introduces MCP

    One common format for plugging any tool into AI. OpenAI and Google later adopt it too.

    In plain wordsUSB-C for AI: any tool plugs in.

  11. 2024.12ChinaDeepSeek V3A 671B-parameter MoE model that gets close to the frontier at a very low training cost.
  12. 2025.01China

    DeepSeek R1 shakes the market

    An open-weight reasoning model that comes close to US flagships. Nvidia's stock plunges in a single day. Many people realize for the first time how close Chinese models are.

  13. 2025.01AgentOpenAI OperatorAI opens a browser to book tables and fill in forms for you. "Doing things" on the web.
  14. 2025.02Agent

    Claude Code preview, and vibe coding

    AI reads and edits files, runs tests and fixes bugs right on your computer. Karpathy coins "vibe coding": building software just by talking.

    In plain wordsThe consultant becomes a tradesperson you invite into your home.

  15. 2025.04AgentOpenAI Codex CLIA coding agent in the terminal becomes standard for every lab.
  16. 2025.05AgentClaude Code becomes generally availableReleased alongside the Claude 4 family.
  17. 2025.06AgentGoogle Gemini CLIAll three big labs now have their own terminal agent.
  18. 2025.07AgentChatGPT agentChatGPT gets its own virtual computer that can browse, build slides and work on spreadsheets.
  19. 2025.08ModelGPT-5OpenAI merges chat and reasoning into one model that decides how long to think.
  20. 2025.09ChinaAnthropic stops serving Chinese-controlled companiesPreventing distillation is one of the stated reasons.
  21. 2025.10AgentAgent SkillsWorkflows written as folders and loaded on demand. "Training your AI" becomes managing text files.
  22. ButlerSend a message, get it doneFrom late 2025, you can get AI to do things from a chat app.
  23. 2025.11ModelGemini 3 and Claude Opus 4.5In the same month, Google's and Anthropic's flagships both push past 80% on the coding exam.
  24. 2025.11AgentThe "lobster" is bornA personal project by engineer Peter Steinberger, with a lobster mascot: send a WhatsApp message and the AI on your home computer does the job. Later renamed OpenClaw.
  25. 2026.01Agent

    OpenClaw goes viral

    Clawdbot → Moltbot → renamed OpenClaw on 1/30, hitting 100,000 stars in days. Fake extensions, scam coins and prompt injection follow.

    In plain wordsTexting your butler, but a butler who's too trusting can also be conned.

  26. 2026.02Agent

    Remote Control: steer the AI on your computer from your phone

    Code and files stay on the computer; the phone is just a remote, with messages relayed through Anthropic.

  27. 2026.02China

    Distillation accusations go public

    OpenAI sends a memo to the US House; Anthropic names DeepSeek, Moonshot and MiniMax, with about 24,000 accounts and 16 million conversations.

  28. 2026.03AgentSending work from your phone becomes a built-in featureAnthropic features like Dispatch and Channels let you hand work to the AI on your computer from your phone or a chat app, without setting anything up yourself.
  29. General contractorA crew of AIs working togetherIn 2026, one person leads a team of AIs.
  30. 2026.04InfrastructureIEA: data center electricity up 17% in a yearElectricity use at AI-focused data centers grows 50%, and is projected to double by 2030.
  31. 2026.06ModelClaude Fable 5Scores 95% on SWE-bench Verified (an exam where AI fixes 500 real software issues). The exam is close to maxed out.
  32. 2026.07ModelGPT-5.6 in three tiers, and Claude Opus 5OpenAI starts splitting each generation into Sol (strongest), Terra (balanced) and Luna (fast and cheap); two weeks later Anthropic releases Opus 5.
  33. 2026.09ModelGPT-6, Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4All three labs ship a new generation in September: GPT-6 Astra (9/3), GPT-6 Sol and Luna on the same day as Opus 5.5 (9/22), and Gemini 4 Argon (9/30).
  34. Fall 2026Agent

    Multi-agent becomes a product feature

    Claude Code launches the new Projects: one coordinating agent talks with you and hands work to threads, each with its own context.

    In plain wordsYou only tell the general contractor what you need; they bring in the plumber and the carpenter to work in parallel.

  35. 2026.10ChinaAnti-distillation measures workAnthropic's second report: the data distillers obtained dropped by about 90–99%.
  36. 2026 —InfrastructureMemory and hard drive shortagesAI data centers buy up HBM, DRAM and hard drive capacity. The next bottlenecks are power and hardware.
