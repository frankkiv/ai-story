True or false?
Eight things people say about AI

Guess first, then read the answer and the reason. No timer, and wrong answers are fine. The explanation is the point.

1 / 8

The longer I chat with an AI, the better it knows me, because it keeps learning from me.

A model does not change because you chat with it. It seems to know you better because the app resends your earlier conversation and memory notes every time.Read the full explanation →

2 / 8

If I turn off memory and start a new chat, the model has no idea what we talked about before.

Correct. That does not mean the record is gone, though: the provider's servers may still hold your chats. To make them disappear, delete them in settings.Read the full explanation →

3 / 8

AI knows recent news because it updates itself every day.

A model is fixed once training ends and does not know what happened afterwards. It can answer because it looks things up on the spot and adds the results to what it reads this turn.Read the full explanation →

4 / 8

If the AI sounds certain, the answer is right.

Sounding confident does not make it right. Double-check numbers, drug names and legal questions; for anything important, check two sources or ask a professional.Read the full explanation →

5 / 8

If one conversation goes on too long, the AI may actually get worse.

It can. There is a limit to how much it can read at once, and with too much unrelated material the important parts get overlooked. Very long chats are also summarized automatically, and details can be lost. A fresh chat is often more accurate.Read the full explanation →

6 / 8

An AI that "does things itself" is the model reaching into the computer and operating it.

The model only outputs a request: "I want to use this tool." A program on your computer actually runs it and hands the result back. That is why the "Allow" step matters.Read the full explanation →

7 / 8

If a vendor says its new model is number one, it is the strongest.

Vendor-reported scores tend to run high, and third-party retests often land a few points lower. And no model is best at everything; it depends on the job.Read the full explanation →

8 / 8

Everything I type will definitely be used to train the next model.

That depends on the service's privacy settings, and most apps let you opt out. Even when chats are used, they affect a new model months later, not the one in front of you learning in real time.Read the full explanation →
